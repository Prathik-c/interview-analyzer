Key Capabilities

Designed to simulate real high-stakes interviews with multimodal AI intelligence.

Resume-Aware Questions

Automatically parses your PDF resume, extracts candidate skills and projects, and tailors questions to your actual background.

Adaptive Technical Interview

Dynamic engine evaluates your answers in real-time and adjusts question difficulty and follow-up depth accordingly.

AI Answer Evaluation

Powered by high-speed Groq NLP to assess technical correctness, concept depth, clarity, and specific domain terminology.

Speech Analysis

Extracts words-per-minute pacing, pauses, and filler word frequency (um, uh, like) to build spoken communication confidence.

Body Language Analysis
